# Crowned Heads — Database Research Batch 18b

A spin-off batch created during the La Aurora research pass (2026-09-30), researched against
https://cigar-site.vercel.app/brands/crowned-heads/.

## Why this exists as its own batch

The 7 photos here were originally filed under "La Aurora/Las Calaveras" on the Mac. Research found this is
actually Crowned Heads' well-known annual Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead) limited edition, first
released 2014, blended by My Father Cigars S.A. in Estelí, Nicaragua. I moved the folder to a new top-level
"Crowned Heads" location. The brand itself already exists on the site (8 other lines: Four Kicks, Four Kicks
Capa Especial, Headley Grange, Jericho Hill, Juárez, Le Carème, Le Pâtissier, Mil Dias) - Las Calaveras is a
new line under that existing brand, not a new brand.

## 1 new line

- `las-calaveras.json` — spans multiple release years rather than one fixed blend, since each year's Las
  Calaveras uses a new wrapper/recipe. Covers the 2023 limited edition (LC52, LC54), the 2024 limited edition
  (LC48, LC52, LC54 - **flag**: the Mac's filenames used shape shorthand like "Corona"/"Robusto"/"Toro" that
  don't match any official "LC" product name, so I mapped them to the closest official size by shape, clearly
  marked as inferred rather than confirmed), and a one-off 2024 10th-anniversary Toro release. A sampler
  photo depicting the real 2024 4-cigar sampler box wasn't added as its own vitola entry since it's a mixed
  multi-blend product.

## Brand

"JFR" stands for **"Just For Retailers"** — originally created to be sold only through brick-and-mortar retailers (no big-box/online-only distribution), which plausibly explains its absence from a general review-site brand index despite being well-documented by halfwheel, Cigar Coop, and Cigar Aficionado. Founded 2005, Nicaraguan origin (Estelí). Historically associated with Casa Fernandez (2013–2016-era sourcing); current (2025) sourcing attributes it to Aganorsa Leaf as a bestselling house brand. Both are Fernandez-family Nicaraguan tobacco operations — no source explicitly narrates the handoff between them, so that transition is recorded as an inference, not a confirmed fact.

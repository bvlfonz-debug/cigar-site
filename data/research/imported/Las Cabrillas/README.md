## Brand background

Distributed by Altadis USA, rolled in Honduras but using an unusual-for-the-region all-Mexican binder/filler
(San Andrés/Tuxtla Valley) — retailers describe this combination as rare. Lines are named after New World
explorers (Balboa, De Soto, Cortez, Columbus, Vasco De Gama). No founding date found.

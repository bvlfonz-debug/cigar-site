# Topper Cigar Company — Database Research Batch 17b

A small spin-off batch created during the J.C. Newman research pass (2026-09-30). This brand has no entry on
the site at all, and its Mac folder didn't exist as a top-level brand until today.

## Why this exists as its own batch

The 2 photos here were originally filed under "J.C. Newman/Topper" on the Mac. Research turned up a clean
correction: Topper Cigar Company is an independent, unrelated 4th-generation family business (founded 1896 in
McSherrystown, PA, run by Chris Topper), manufactured in Danli, Honduras. It does not appear anywhere in J.C.
Newman's own brand list or Wikipedia page. I moved the folder to its own top-level location
(`Cigar Images/Topper Cigar Company/Topper Danli/`) and gave it a full brand entry here rather than leaving
it misattributed.

## 1 new brand, 1 new line

- `topper-danli.json` — full brand object (Topper Cigar Company) plus its "Topper Danli" line, a sub-blend
  distinct from the flagship "Topper 1894" line. Only 1 vitola was photographed (Toro, 6x50); three more real
  Danli vitolas (Robusto, Churchill, Belicoso) are confirmed at retail but not photographed, so they're noted
  but not formally added.

## Sourcing

Famous Smoke, Cigar Storehouse, Best Cigar Prices, Cigar Coop, and J.C. Newman's own Wikipedia page (used to
confirm the non-affiliation).

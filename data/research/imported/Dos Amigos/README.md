# Dos Amigos — Database Research Batch (2026-10-11)

## Context

Dos Amigos is **not on the site at all** — confirmed absent from the site's full 112-brand index. A full new `brand` object is included. Confirmed as a genuinely **separate, distinct brand** from the site's existing unrelated "Dos Hermanos" listing and from Drew Estate's "ACID Amigos" line — not a misremembering of either.

## Brand

A brand-new (2025) brand conceived in Barcelona, Spain by founders Jordi Llorens(-Carrió) and Isidre Pineda, with cigars produced in Nicaragua. Officially launched internationally at InterTabac 2025. Covered so far mainly by European trade press (Cigar Journal, Cigar Smoker Club) rather than US cigar media, which is consistent with its absence from this site's index.

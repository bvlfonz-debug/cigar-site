# Blue Eyed Jack — Database Research Batch (2026-10-11)

## Context

The Mac folder is named "Blue Eyed Jack," but research found that **this is the line name, not the brand/company name**. The actual brand is **ADVentura** (made/distributed by ADV & McKay Cigars Co.), which is not on the site at all — confirmed absent from the full 112-brand index. A full new `brand` object is included.

## Brand

ADVentura, founded 2016 by Henderson Ventura (of Tabacalera William Ventura) and Marcel Knobel (Swiss sommelier/coffee producer-retailer). Sold in Europe from 2017; US market debut around 2019 (IPCPR). Manufactured at Tabacalera Mina del Rey, Dominican Republic, co-founded by Ventura and Knobel in late 2023.

## Line added (1)

**ADVentura Blue Eyed Jack's Revenge** (`adventura-blue-eyed-jacks-revenge.json`) — Corona 6x44, Robusto 5x52, Maduro "High Tide" (Diadema) 6.5x50.

Confirmed as **one line** with a base blend plus a Maduro wrapper variant sub-named "High Tide" — not two separate lines. Uses a "talking box" gimmick, also used on ADVentura's earlier Barbarroja's Invasion (2022).

**Flag:** the filler composition has a one-source conflict — Cigar Coop's own article has an internal inconsistency (its blend-summary box says "Dominican filler," its body text says "aged Nicaraguan fillers"), while 3 other sources agree on Dominican only. Recorded as Dominican with the conflict noted rather than silently resolved.

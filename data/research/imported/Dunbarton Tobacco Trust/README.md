# Dunbarton Tobacco & Trust — Database Research Batch 16

Researched against the live catalog at https://cigar-site.vercel.app/brands/dunbarton-tobacco-trust/ on
2026-09-30. Covers everything except Mi Querida and its sub-lines (Black, Triqui Traca), which were already
researched separately under the Aganorsa Leaf batch earlier in this project.

## Small batch, well-organized folder, two solid gap-fills plus one new line

Founded 2013 by Steve Saka after he left Drew Estate (where he was President/CEO) - independent, small-batch,
Danbury CT-based, Nicaragua-sourced. No ownership tie to Drew Estate or Room101 despite the alumnus
connection; well-regarded across the major review outlets.

## 1 new line

- `sobremesa-brulee.json` — Dunbarton's own site treats "Sobremesa Brûlée" as its own distinct marca, not
  just a wrapper variant of core Sobremesa - it's a real recipe change (less ligero, more Condega Seco) with a
  noticeably different, milder profile. The Mac photo here is a generic marketing banner rather than a
  per-vitola shot, so the vitola list reflects the line's full published lineup rather than one confirmed
  photo. Two further limited sub-variants (Brûlée Blue, Brûlée Wagashi) are flagged but not added - couldn't
  confirm current specs/availability beyond passing retailer mentions.

## 2 gap-fill supplements

- `_stillwell-star-additional-vitolas.json` — site lists only "Aromatic No. 1." Adds "Bayou No. 32," a real,
  verified blend in the line (all StillWell Star vitolas share a 6x52 toro format). A second Mac photo,
  "Stillwell_23," couldn't be matched to any documented blend number in the line (confirmed numbers are 1,
  22, 27, 32, 1056) - left out rather than guessed, flagged in case it's a misread of 22 or 27.
- `_umbagog-additional-vitolas.json` — site lists only "Corona Gorda." Adds "Bronzeback Robusto" - **flag
  worth your attention**: I inferred this from Dunbarton's naming conventions elsewhere, since no source
  directly documents the shorthand filename itself ("Bb-Rob"). The underlying product (Umbagog's Bronzeback
  sub-blend, a different Broadleaf priming, released ~June 2025, in a Robusto size) is real and verified - the
  match to this specific photo is the part that's a strong inference rather than a confirmed identification.

## 2 folders with no actionable gap

- **Sin Compromiso** and **Muestra de Saka** each have only a generic box/logo photo with no specific vitola
  name visible, so neither could be cross-referenced against the site's existing single-vitola listings.

## Sourcing

Facts came from Dunbarton's own site (dunbartoncigars.com), halfwheel, Cigar Coop, and several retailers (JR
Cigars, Cigars International, Atlantic Cigar, Smoke Inn, Leaf Enthusiast, Developing Palates, Stogie Press).

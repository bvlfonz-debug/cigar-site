# La Aurora — Database Research Batch 18

Researched against the live catalog at https://cigar-site.vercel.app/brands/la-aurora/ on 2026-09-30.

## Big finding: "Las Calaveras" isn't a La Aurora product

The Mac's "La Aurora" folder had a 7-photo "Las Calaveras" subfolder, but research turned up a clean
correction: Las Calaveras is **Crowned Heads'** annual Día de los Muertos limited edition, blended by My
Father Cigars in Estelí - La Aurora has no involvement. I moved the folder to its own new top-level "Crowned
Heads" location and researched it as its own batch (see that folder's separate README) - Crowned Heads
already exists on the site with 8 other lines, so Las Calaveras slots in as a new line under the existing
brand rather than a whole new brand entry.

## 1 gap-fill supplement

- `_la-aurora-107-additional-vitolas.json` — the site lists only one vitola ("Ecuador Robusto," 5x50) for
  the 107 line. Added Toro, Gran Toro, and Belicoso, verified off Cigars International's current product
  listing. The Mac's own photos for this line are a generic variety-pack group shot plus one single-cigar
  photo whose exact vitola I couldn't pin down - possibly Gran Toro based on the filename, but flagged as
  unverified rather than asserted. Two further sub-line-specific vitolas (Lancero, Zeppelin - apparently
  exclusive to "107 Ecuador") and two older names from a 2012 review (Corona, Gran 107, likely discontinued)
  were left out rather than added on weaker evidence.

# Domain — Database Research Batch (2026-10-11)

## Context

Domain Cigars is **not on the site at all** — confirmed absent from the site's full 112-brand index. A full new `brand` object is included.

## Important correction

The starting hypothesis was that "Domain" might be a line of the already-listed brand AJ Fernandez. Research ruled this out — **Domain is its own standalone brand**, founded March 2024 by Daniel Lance (cigar retail background, founder of Lujo Concepts) and Esteban Disla (cigar-making veteran, ~10 years at Fábrica de Tabacos Nica Sueño S.A.; earlier created blends for CAO, Toraño, and Alec Bradley). Its absence from the site's index is consistent with it being a genuinely new brand, not evidence it's fictional.

## Line added (1)

**Domain Entropy** (`domain-entropy.json`) — Toro 6x50, Robusto Extra 5x52, Robusto Gordo 5x56. Domain's third regular-production line (after Neutron and Negentropy), a refined evolution of an earlier limited "No7B" prototype.

**Flag:** the Mac photo `Domain_Entropy-Robx_01.jpg` is ambiguous between the line's "Robusto Extra" (5x52) and "Robusto Gordo" (5x56) — no source uses "Robx" as an official abbreviation for either. Both vitolas are documented in the line entry since both are confirmed real, but the specific photo isn't attributed to either with confidence.

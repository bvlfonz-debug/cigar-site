# Charter Oak — Database Research Batch (2026-10-11)

## Context

Charter Oak is **not on the site at all** — confirmed absent from the site's full brand index. This is a brand-new addition, discovered as a byproduct of this session's Camacho gap-fill research rather than targeted from the start.

## Discovery / correction

4 Mac photos were originally sitting in the `Camacho` folder with filenames referencing "Charter Oak" ("Ch-Broad-Tor," "Charter-Oak-Broad-Toro," "Charter-Oak-CT-Roth," "Charter-Oak-CT-Toro"). Rather than assume these were a Camacho sub-line because of the folder they were found in, dedicated research was done into what "Charter Oak" actually is. It turned out to have **no corporate or production connection to Camacho or Davidoff at all** — it's made by **Foundation Cigar Company**, founded by Nicholas Melillo (former Drew Estate master blender).

The 4 files were physically moved out of `Camacho` into a newly created top-level `Charter Oak` folder (2 line subfolders: "Charter Oak Connecticut Shade" and "Charter Oak Connecticut Broadleaf") and renamed with corrected, descriptive filenames reflecting the real brand/line/vitola. This mirrors the pattern established earlier in the session for other misfiled spin-off brands (Topper Cigar Company, Crowned Heads/Las Calaveras).

A full new `brand` object is included (see `charter-oak-connecticut-shade.json`) since the brand does not exist on the site at all.

## Lines added (2)

1. **Charter Oak Connecticut Shade** (`charter-oak-connecticut-shade.json`) — Toro 6x52, Rothschild 4.25x50. A budget-friendly, mild-to-medium Foundation line.
2. **Charter Oak Connecticut Broadleaf** (`charter-oak-connecticut-broadleaf.json`) — Toro 6x52 (the only vitola found matching a Mac photo this pass; also referred to as "Charter Oak Maduro" by some retailers). A fuller-bodied maduro-wrapper counterpart to the Shade version.

## What wasn't confirmed this session

Binder, filler, and factory details are left `null` for both lines — not confirmed in available sourcing this pass, recorded honestly rather than guessed. The brand's specific Charter Oak sub-brand launch date is also unconfirmed (Foundation Cigar Company's own founding is documented, but not a precise Charter Oak launch date).

The Connecticut Broadleaf line likely has more vitolas than the single Toro found in the Mac photos — not independently researched beyond what the photos required this pass.

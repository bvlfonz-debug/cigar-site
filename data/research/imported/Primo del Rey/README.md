# Primo del Rey — Database Research Batch (Altadis USA reorganization, part 12)

Checked against the site's live brand index on 2026-10-11 — **Primo del Rey is confirmed completely absent**.

## Important correction to an initial assumption

Research found this brand is **Honduran-made**, not Dominican, and **no source ties it to Altadis USA** or
any other named distributor — every retailer source found (JR Cigars, Corona Cigar) simply sells it without
naming a parent company. No founding date, founder, or origin story could be found anywhere, and no
Wikipedia page exists for the brand. The `brand` object records distributor as unknown rather than asserting
the unconfirmed Altadis USA attribution. Only 2 active retailers were found carrying this brand at all,
suggesting a small or declining distribution footprint (cause not confirmed).

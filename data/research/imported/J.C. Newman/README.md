# J.C. Newman — Database Research Batch 17

Researched against the live catalog at https://cigar-site.vercel.app/brands/jc-newman/ on 2026-09-30.

## Big finding: "Topper" isn't a J.C. Newman product at all

The Mac's "J.C. Newman" folder had a "Topper" subfolder, but research turned up a clean, well-sourced
correction: **Topper Cigar Company is a separate, unrelated manufacturer** - an independent 4th-generation
family business founded 1896 in Pennsylvania, run by Chris Topper. It doesn't appear anywhere in J.C.
Newman's own brand list or Wikipedia page. I moved the folder out to its own top-level "Topper Cigar Company"
location and researched it as its own brand - see that folder's separate README.

## The site's J.C. Newman entry only covers 3 of the company's lines

It lists El Reloj, The American, and Yagua. Four more real, current lines turned up in the Mac photos.

## 4 new lines

- `brick-house.json` — the company's value-priced Nicaraguan line. 2 vitolas (Classic Robusto, Classic
  Toro). One minor size discrepancy flagged: JR Cigars lists Robusto at 5x54, while J.C. Newman's own site
  review page says 5x50 for the same vitola - recorded both, didn't pick arbitrarily.
- `brick-house-double-connecticut.json` — **treated as its own line, not folded into plain Brick House**,
  since it has a genuinely different wrapper (Connecticut Shade vs. Ecuador Habano-seed) and profile. The Mac
  photo labeled "CT-Mighty" decoded to "Mighty Mighty" (6.25x60) - the real name repeats the word, it's not
  just "Mighty."
- `diamond-crown.json` — the company's flagship long-aged Dominican prestige line. 2 vitolas (No. 4, No. 5).
  One photo ("3-Pack 2024") turned out to depict a real but different product - a holiday sampler of Cameroon
  and MAXIMUS Belicosos, not the No.4/No.5 vitolas - flagged rather than misattributed.
- `el-baton.json` — the company's first all-Nicaraguan puro, originally a 1914 nickel cigar, revived 2008
  and relaunched 2022/2024. 4 vitolas (Belicoso, Double Toro, Double Torpedo, Robusto), all cleanly verified
  off J.C. Newman's own site.

## 1 gap-fill supplement

- `_the-american-additional-vitolas.json` — adds Torpedo, a real current vitola confirmed on J.C. Newman's
  own product page. Sizing has drifted slightly across years/retailers (6.125x52 to 6.5x52) - recorded the
  verified range rather than picking one figure. A further vitola, "President" (7x47), is also real per the
  manufacturer's site but has no corresponding Mac photo, so it's flagged but not added.

## Sourcing

Facts came heavily from J.C. Newman's own site (jcnewman.com), plus halfwheel, Cigar Aficionado, Cigar Coop,
and retailers (JR Cigars, Neptune Cigar, Corona Cigar, Smoke Inn, LM Cigars). One claim I could NOT verify:
a commonly-assumed Ernesto Perez-Carrillo connection to Diamond Crown - flagged as unconfirmed in that file
rather than stated as fact.

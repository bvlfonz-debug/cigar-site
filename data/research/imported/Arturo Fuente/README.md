# Arturo Fuente — Database Research Batch 14

Researched against the live catalog at https://cigar-site.vercel.app/brands/arturo-fuente/ on 2026-09-30.

## No new lines this batch — all gaps were additional vitolas on existing lines

Every line photographed on the Mac already exists on the site (Casa Cuba, Gran Reserva, Fuente Fuente OpusX,
plus three lines with no actionable gap - see below). The site's entries for two lines turned out to be
significantly incomplete, though.

## Folder cleanup first

6 files sat loose at the top level. 5 were Fuente Fuente OpusX box-open photos with cryptically abbreviated
filenames (e.g. "Opus-Angel-Rob", "Opus-Sup-Beli") - I ran these through research to decode them, then moved
them into the existing "Fuente Fuente OpusX" folder with clear names. The 6th was a "Father and Son" sampler
photo claiming a 26-cigar count I couldn't verify anywhere (most documented editions of this recurring annual
Fuente sampler are 5- or 10-cigar boxes) - renamed in place with that flag, left at the top level since it
doesn't clearly belong in any of the 6 line folders. Two untouched press-magazine PNGs were left alone, per
this project's usual convention.

I also corrected the 6 vitola photo filenames inside the existing "Casa Cuba" folder - they'd been filed under
bare Spanish numbers ("Cinco," "Dos," "Tres," "Quatro," "Seis") plus one outright wrong word ("Divine"), none
of which are the cigars' real official names (see below).

## 3 gap-fill supplements

- `_casa-cuba-additional-vitolas.json` — **the site was missing 5 of 6 photographed vitolas.** It lists only
  "Doble Cinco" (5x50). Research confirmed Casa Cuba's real vitola names all carry a "Doble" prefix that the
  Mac's abbreviated filenames had dropped (Doble Dos, Doble Cuatro, Doble Tres, Doble Seis), and that "Divine"
  should actually be "Divine Inspiration" (an English phrase, not "Divino" as I'd initially guessed before
  research corrected me). All 5 additions are verified against Fuente's own site and halfwheel. I also learned
  Casa Cuba isn't a Perez-Carrillo collaboration as I'd initially assumed - it's a revived 1956 Ybor City brand
  Fuente acquired in the 1970s and relaunched in 2013 with an original Carlos Fuente Sr. blend.
- `_gran-reserva-additional-vitolas.json` — **the site was missing 8 of 9 photographed vitolas.** It lists
  only the "Flor Fina 8-5-8." All 8 additional Mac-photographed vitolas (Rothschild, Petit Corona, Cuban
  Corona, Spanish Lonsdale, Corona Imperial, Seleccion Privada No. 1, Churchill, Canones) checked out as real,
  currently-catalogued sizes straight from Fuente's own site - this looks like the site's Gran Reserva entry
  was simply never fully populated. Four more vitola names (Brevas Royale, Exquisitos, Cazadores, Curly Head
  Deluxe) turned up in secondary-retailer references but weren't verifiable against a primary source and have
  no corresponding Mac photo, so they're flagged in the file but not added.
- `_opusx-additional-vitolas.json` — 4 limited/special OpusX releases not on the site's 8-vitola core list:
  Angel's Share Robusto, Belicoso XXX, The Undefeated (a Cigar Rights of America charity-sampler exclusive -
  confirmed this has nothing to do with a boxer or the "Undefeated" streetwear brand, despite what the name
  might suggest), and the 25th Anniversary Churchill. One photo (labeled just "Oro") is left as an open,
  unresolved flag rather than guessed at - two different real OpusX releases use that word and I can't tell
  which without eyes on the actual band/box art.

## 3 folders with no actionable gap

- **Añejo Reserva** and **Hemingway** each have only one generic, vitola-agnostic product photo plus a stock
  band image - nothing to cross-reference against the site's 7-vitola listings for either line.
- **Don Carlos** has no per-vitola product photos at all, only people/marketing shots (Carlos Fuente Jr. and
  Miguel Angel Garcia, plus two photos of unclear subjects) - nothing to check against the site's 9-vitola
  listing.

## Sourcing

Facts came from Arturo Fuente's own site (arturofuente.com), halfwheel, Cigar Aficionado, Cigar Coop, and
several retailers (Neptune Cigar, Corona Cigar, Cigars Direct) for cross-confirmation. One minor size
discrepancy (Casa Cuba Doble Dos: 6.75x42 per Fuente's own site vs. 6.5x42 per halfwheel's review) is flagged
in that file rather than picked arbitrarily.

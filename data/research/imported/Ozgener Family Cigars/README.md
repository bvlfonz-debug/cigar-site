# Ozgener Family Cigars — Database Research Batch 13

Researched against the live catalog at https://cigar-site.vercel.app/brands/ozgener/ on 2026-09-30. (Note: the
brand's site slug is `/brands/ozgener/`, not `/brands/ozgener-family-cigars/` — the latter 404s.)

## Folder reorganization first — a whole new line was hiding at the top level

20 files sat loose, unorganized, at the top of Ozgener's Mac folder. I sorted them: 8 belonged to a line not
represented in any folder at all, and 12 were duplicate/complementary photos (box-open shots, or a second
single-stick angle) for vitolas already covered in the existing Bosphorus and Firsat folders — those got
merged in. See "Karatoba" below for the new line.

## Background: this is a real, currently active small brand

Founded 2022 by Tim Ozgener (previously of CAO, his father's company), now generally marketed as "OZ Family
Cigars," distributed by Crowned Heads. Core lines (Bosphorus, Aramas, Firsat) are made at Casa Carrillo/
Tabacalera La Alianza in the Dominican Republic; the newer Karatoba line is made at My Father Cigars in
Nicaragua. Not connected to the historic CAO brand beyond the founder's family background.

## 1 new line: Karatoba

`karatoba.json` — **naming flag worth your attention.** The Mac photos for this line were filed under just
"Kari," with no other identifying text, and no product literally named "Kari" exists anywhere I could find.
But a real Ozgener line called "Karatoba" has an exact match for all 4 photographed vitola names (Gordo,
Robusto Extra, Robusto, Toro) — no other line in the brand's catalog matches that set. I've renamed the Mac
folder from "Ozgener Kari" to "Ozgener Karatoba" and filed this under that name, but recommend a quick visual
check of the box art to confirm before treating it as certain.

  (5.5x55), F60 (6x60), all verified.

## Sourcing

Since this is a small, newer brand, coverage is thinner than the larger brands in this project. Facts came
primarily from Cigar Coop, Cigar Aficionado, halfwheel, and the brand's own current site (ozfamilycigars.com),
supplemented by a few retailers (Atlantic Cigar, Neptune Cigar, Holt's) where review coverage was sparse.

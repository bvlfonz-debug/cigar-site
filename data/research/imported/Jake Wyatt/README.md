# Jake Wyatt — Database Research Batch 21

Researched against the live catalog at https://cigar-site.vercel.app/brands/jake-wyatt/ on 2026-09-30.

## A small boutique brand with thin, occasionally contradictory sourcing

Jake Wyatt is a small-batch brand (named after the founders' sons) with little third-party editorial
coverage - no halfwheel, Cigar Coop, or Cigar Aficionado review confirmed current specs for either line
researched here. More notably, the brand's **own website gave inconsistent details across different page
pulls** - binder/filler origin for Appendix II, and wrapper leaf entirely for Lithium (which also appears to
have been resized since its 2020 halfwheel-reviewed debut). I've flagged all of this rather than picking one
version arbitrarily.

## 2 gap-fill supplements

- `_appendix-ii-additional-vitolas.json` — site lists only Toro (6x54), matching a Mac photo exactly. Adds
  Corona Gorda (the Mac photo said just "Corona," but the real product name includes "Gorda"), Robusto, and
  Gordo.
- `_lithium-additional-vitolas.json` — site lists only Toro (6x54). Adds Belicoso and Robusto. Wrapper leaf
  is disputed across sources (Sumatra vs. Ecuadorian Habano) - flagged rather than resolved.

## Not photographed

Three more lines mentioned on the site's brand overview (U.S.I.R., Fourth Dimension, Lucid Interval, Herbert
Spencer) have no Mac photos, so no action was taken on them.

## Sourcing

Jake Wyatt's own site (jakewyattcigars.com), Famous Smoke, and halfwheel (for Lithium's original 2020
specs, used mainly to flag the apparent resizing rather than as the current authoritative size).

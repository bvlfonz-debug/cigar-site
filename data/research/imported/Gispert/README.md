photo, 2-retailer confirmed) and Robusto are solidly sourced; Toro, Corona, and Belicoso are single-retailer

collaboration) was identified as a genuinely distinct product from the core line — not included in this batch
since there's no supporting Mac photo, but flagged for a future pass if photos surface.
